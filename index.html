Privacy Policy – Marble Solitaire

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Marble Solitaire — also published as Senku, Resta Um and Solitaer — is a one-player jump-and-remove puzzle. Marble Solitaire does not collect, store, or share any personal data directly. The game does not require user registration and does not collect personal information such as name, email address, or location.

However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information automatically to provide their services.

Advertising

Marble Solitaire uses third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to display interstitial and rewarded ads. These services may collect certain data automatically, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage data

This information is managed directly by the advertising provider and is subject to their respective privacy policies. You can review Google's advertising practices here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before any personalised advertising identifier is used, and may withdraw or change that consent at any time from the app's settings.

Analytics

Marble Solitaire uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as game events and session information, that help us improve the game. This data is associated with a randomly generated identifier and does not include personal information such as name, email address, or precise location.

Google Play Services

The app may use Google Play Services, which may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. For more information, please review:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Local Data

Game progress, scores, statistics, streaks and settings are stored locally on your device. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. There is no account, no cloud save and no server: the game works fully offline.

Children's Privacy

Marble Solitaire is not specifically directed at children under 13 years of age. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the Effective Date above will be updated.
